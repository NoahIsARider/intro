One hand-painted plate for a faded children's encyclopedia that has been left in the damp. Landscape 3:2. Gouache and thin ink on coarse paper, painted by a patient amateur illustrator. Palette: peat brown, bog green, dirty cream, a note of rust, everything dulled by damp. Visible paper grain, pigment mottling, small foxing spots, chalky muted pigment, a fine dark vignette at the four edges, no digital smoothness and no photographic depth of field.

SCENE
a waterlogged clearing at the edge of a birch bog, seen from standing height. Low hummocks of moss, standing black water in the hollows, a ring of pale toadstools at the middle distance, a dead silver birch at the left edge, ground mist lying in the dips. Nothing alive is visible. The mood is the minute after someone has walked away.

COMPOSITION RULE
Keep a quiet, almost empty band of sky and mist across the upper third where a title will sit, with nothing of interest there. Treat empty ground as the subject. At most one large element and a few small ones. Everything sits low and flat, like a staging set rather than a photograph.

FORBIDDEN
Figures, people, animals with faces, lettering, numbers, signature, watermark, border, frame, sun disc, moon disc, lens flare, bokeh, HDR, neon, highly saturated colour, anime, digital painting gloss, symmetrical wallpaper pattern.
