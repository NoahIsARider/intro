One hand-painted plate for a faded children's encyclopedia that has been left in the damp. Landscape 3:2. Gouache and thin ink on coarse paper, painted by a patient amateur illustrator. Palette: bone cream, graphite grey, sepia, one note of rust. Visible paper grain, pigment mottling, small foxing spots, chalky muted pigment, a fine dark vignette at the four edges, no digital smoothness and no photographic depth of field.

SCENE
a wet moor at night, the ground a flat grey-green wash, a long low ridge on the horizon, a standing stone doorway in the right third catching the light, thin mist lying across the ground, a narrow path worn through the grass toward it. Low moon glow behind the mist but no moon disc.

COMPOSITION RULE
Keep a quiet, almost empty band of sky across the upper two thirds where a title will sit, with nothing of interest there. Treat empty ground as the subject. At most one large element and a few small ones. Everything sits low and flat, like a staging set rather than a photograph.
